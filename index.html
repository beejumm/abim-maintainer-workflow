Stage 3

Discuss on AnkiHub Community

Every suggestion gets a thread. The thread is where the vote happens.

Tag @abim-maintainers when: you are proposing a new tag · sources conflict or the guideline is ambiguous · the change is structural (merging, splitting, renumbering clozes, retiring a card) · the card touches a contested or rapidly changing area · you are unsure about anything at all. Nobody is penalised for asking.
Stage 4

Vote and approval

A change is approved when it has upvotes from 50% of maintainers, rounded down.

Appendix A

Tag reference

Blueprint tags all live under one root: #AK_ABIM::!Specialties::<Subject>::NN_<Topic>::NN_<Subtopic>

#AK_ABIM::!Specialties::Infectious_Disease::05_Lower_respiratory_tract_infections::02_Pneumonia
#AK_ABIM::!Specialties::Cardiovascular_Disease::15_Heart_failure::02_Heart_failure_with_reduced_ejection_fraction
#AK_ABIM::!Specialties::Preventive_Medicine::01_Cancer_screening::04_Colorectal_cancer_screening
#AK_ABIM::!Specialties::Medical_Oncology::07_Bone_tumors
#AK_ABIM::MKSAP::abxyz12345

Some branches stop at the topic level and have no subtopic — that is normal. Copy verbatim, never delete an existing tag, and use the deepest blueprint tag that fits.

Appendix C

Why suggestions get rejected

  1. Duplicate — Stage 1 searches were skipped or too narrow
  2. Missing source QID tag
  3. Blueprint tag too vague, or a new tag invented without a vote
  4. Content added from outside the screenshot
  5. Condition name clozed away, so the stem gives no context
  6. Clozes renumbered unnecessarily, destroying subscribers' review history
  7. Extra bullets padded with material from incorrect answer options
  8. Card overloaded — three facts crammed into one sentence instead of split
  9. No Missed Question screenshot, or an uncropped full-page one
  10. Merged or edited by the submitter without a vote
